Andrea Ninni

PostDoc ResearcherUniversity of Rome Tor Vergata · Integrative Physiology and Metabolism Laboratory

I study how adipose tissue adapts to its environment and the role of macrophages in this role, using single-cell and bulk transcriptomics. When the analysis I need does not exist, I build it: the latest example is MACanalyzeR, an R package for profiling monocytes and macrophages in single-cell RNA-seq data.

Read the Nature Communication paper MACanalyzeR Vignette CV

Department of Biology, University of Rome Tor Vergata, 00133 Rome, Italy
Supervisor: Daniele Lettieri Barbato

What I work on

  • Adipose tissue and its environment

    How brown and white fat remodel under metabolic stress such as obesity, mapped cell by cell with single-cell and bulk RNA-seq.

  • Macrophages and immunometabolism

    Lipid-associated macrophages in brown fat, what they secrete, and how they change the identity of the cells around them.

  • Tools for transcriptomics

    R packages and machine-learning models that turn single-cell data into answers, with an eye on deep learning for microscopy images.
